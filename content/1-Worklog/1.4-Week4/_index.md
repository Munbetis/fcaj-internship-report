### Week 4 Objectives:

* Learn about Identity & Security in AWS according to the AWS Study Group roadmap.
* Reinforce knowledge of AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM), IAM Users, Groups, and Roles.
* Learn about IAM Policies, Permissions, and the Least Privilege principle.
* Study methods for managing access permissions and securing accounts using MFA.
* Learn about the requirements and prepare an outline for a website-based Workshop report.
* Practice the Secure Multi-user Environment lab to reinforce knowledge of IAM and security.

### Week 4 Achievements:

* Reinforced knowledge of **AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)**:
  * Understood the role of IAM in identity management and access control.
  * Distinguished between IAM Users, Groups, and Roles.
  * Understood how to organize and manage multiple users in an AWS environment.

* Learned about **IAM Policies and Permissions**:
  * Understood the structure of an IAM Policy, including Effect, Action, Resource, and Condition.
  * Distinguished between AWS Managed Policies and Customer Managed Policies.
  * Understood the Least Privilege principle in access management.

* Gained knowledge of **managing and testing access permissions**:
  * Learned the process of creating IAM Users and IAM Groups.
  * Understood how to assign IAM Policies to users or groups.
  * Became familiar with the IAM Policy Simulator and the use of IAM Roles to grant temporary permissions.

* Reinforced knowledge of **MFA and Account Security**:
  * Understood the role of Multi-Factor Authentication in account protection.
  * Learned the process of setting up Virtual MFA.
  * Understood the principles of protecting AWS login credentials.

* **Prepared the Workshop report at the company**:
  * Learned about preparing a Workshop report in the form of a website.
  * Reviewed the structure and requirements of the AWS Study Group sample report.
  * Began outlining the content and identifying the sections to be implemented in the next stages.

* **Hands-on - Secure Multi-user Environment** (scheduled for October 17, 2026):
  * Practiced managing IAM Users, Groups, and Policies.
  * Configured appropriate access permissions for each user group.
  * Set up MFA and tested IAM Users' access permissions.
  * Reinforced skills in identity management, access control, and security in an AWS environment.
