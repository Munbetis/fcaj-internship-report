### Week 1 Objectives:

* Become familiar with the internship environment and the AWS Study Group program.
* Learn about Cloud Computing and basic AWS services.
* Become familiar with the AWS Management Console.
* Learn about account security, IAM, and AWS cost management.
* Participate in learning activities and technology events.

### Week 1 Achievements:

* Gained an overview of **Cloud Computing** and **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**, including the basic service categories:
  * Compute
  * Storage
  * Networking
  * Database

* Became familiar with the **AWS Management Console** and understood how to:
  * Search for and access AWS services.
  * Check account information.
  * Monitor and manage AWS resources.

* Understood the basic principles of **AWS Account Security**:
  * The role of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  * Methods for protecting AWS accounts.
  * The importance of preventing unauthorized access.

* Gained foundational knowledge of **AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)**:
  * Distinguish between IAM Users, Groups, Roles, and Policies.
  * Understand identity management and access permissions.
  * Learn about the process of creating an Admin Group and assigning permissions to users.

* Gained basic knowledge of **AWS Billing & Cost Management**:
  * Learned how to set up AWS Budgets.
  * Understood how to monitor costs and use Billing Alerts.
  * Learned important considerations when using AWS Credits to minimize unnecessary expenses.

* Participated in the **BUILDRATHON KICKOFF: CODE THE FUTURE WITH CMC GLOBAL** event:
  * Gained exposure to activities related to programming and product development.
  * Learned about the technology industry and innovation.
  * Recorded useful knowledge to support academic learning and internship activities.

* Completed the study of foundational topics during the first week, establishing a basis for further learning and hands-on practice with AWS services in the following weeks.
