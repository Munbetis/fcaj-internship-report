# INTERNSHIP SELF-ASSESSMENT

## 1. Introduction

During my participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship program, I have had the opportunity to explore cloud computing technologies through the Amazon Web Services (AWS) ecosystem.

Research activities, hands-on exercises, and report preparation have helped me apply the knowledge acquired at university to the study of practical technology solutions.

In particular, I have focused on researching services such as Amazon DynamoDB, AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, and Amazon S3, while also exploring Serverless architecture and the application of AWS in library management systems.

Additionally, I have participated in researching and preparing materials for the group project **Cloud Library Management System**, as well as developing technical blog content about Amazon DynamoDB, AWS Cost Optimization, and Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Through these activities, I have gradually improved my technical knowledge, independent learning ability, problem-solving skills, and sense of responsibility.

To reflect on my learning and internship progress, I have conducted a self-assessment based on criteria related to technical competence, work attitude, learning ability, and collaboration skills.

## 3. Strengths

### 3.1. Independent Learning and Research

One of my strengths is my willingness to explore and learn new concepts independently.

When studying AWS services, I try to research documentation, understand the purpose of each service, and practice using the technologies to gain a clearer understanding.

In particular, while studying Amazon DynamoDB, I have had the opportunity to explore NoSQL data organization, database operations, and data modeling approaches suitable for application requirements.

Preparing technical blog posts has also helped me improve my ability to summarize, organize, and present knowledge systematically.

### 3.2. Proactiveness and Responsibility

During the internship, I have tried to actively understand the requirements of assigned tasks and organize my work to complete them.

When encountering difficulties during hands-on exercises, I prefer reviewing the implementation steps, investigating the causes, and testing appropriate solutions before seeking additional support.

I also recognize the importance of tracking learning progress, preparing internship reports, and completing tasks within my responsibilities.

Although I still need to improve my time management skills, I believe that my proactive attitude and sense of responsibility have contributed positively to my progress.

### 3.3. Willingness to Improve

I am motivated to expand my knowledge and strengthen skills that still need improvement.

While researching technical topics, I have realized that learning an unfamiliar technology often requires time, experimentation, and troubleshooting.

Instead of focusing only on the final result, I try to understand the implementation process so that I can apply the knowledge to similar situations in the future.

I am also willing to receive feedback and use it to improve my learning methods, presentation skills, and problem-solving approaches.

### 3.4. Professional Attitude

I aim to maintain a serious, respectful, and polite attitude toward others throughout the internship program.

During communication and collaboration, I recognize the importance of listening, respecting different opinions, and maintaining a cooperative mindset.

These qualities are important for developing professional behavior in future working environments.

## 4. Areas for Improvement

### 4.1. Professional Knowledge and Technical Skills

Although I have explored several AWS services and practiced database operations, I recognize that my technical knowledge still needs further development.

In particular, I need to strengthen my ability to integrate multiple AWS services into a complete system, understand security mechanisms, and troubleshoot issues during deployment.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Continue practicing with AWS services I have studied.
- Read more official technical documentation.
- Complete additional labs integrating multiple services.
- Improve my testing and validation skills.

### 4.2. Communication and Presentation Skills

I recognize that I need to improve my ability to present information in a concise, clear, and well-organized manner.

When explaining technical concepts, I especially need to practice using accurate terminology and communicating ideas in ways that are easy for others to understand.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Participate more actively in discussions.
- Practice presenting technical topics I have learned.
- Prepare key points before giving reports.
- Use feedback to improve communication.

### 4.3. Teamwork Skills

While researching the Cloud Library Management System project, I have recognized that collaboration among team members is important for maintaining progress and consistency.

I need to continue improving how I communicate progress, coordinate implementation approaches, and collaborate when my work depends on other components.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Establish clear objectives and responsibilities before starting tasks.
- Provide regular progress updates.
- Communicate difficulties early.
- Improve collaboration during system design and integration.

### 4.4. Problem-Solving Skills

When encountering technical errors or unfamiliar issues, I sometimes need additional time to identify their causes and determine suitable solutions.

Therefore, I would like to improve my ability to analyze problems systematically instead of trying multiple approaches without understanding the underlying cause.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Carefully review error messages and relevant documentation.
- Divide complex problems into smaller tasks.
- Test possible causes systematically.
- Document errors and their solutions.
- Apply lessons learned to future situations.

### 4.5. Discipline and Time Management

During the internship, I need to balance learning AWS, completing hands-on exercises, researching the group project, and preparing reports.

Because multiple tasks must be handled simultaneously, I recognize the need for more detailed planning to monitor my progress effectively.

**Improvement Plan:**

- Create weekly work plans.
- Prioritize tasks based on importance and deadlines.
- Divide major assignments into smaller objectives.
- Review progress regularly.
- Allocate additional time for unexpected technical issues.

## 5. Overall Assessment

Based on my learning progress and the activities completed during the current stage of the internship, I assess my overall performance as **Fair**.

I believe that I have demonstrated a willingness to learn, a positive attitude, and a proactive approach to researching AWS technologies.

Through hands-on activities and preparation for the Cloud Library Management System project, I have had opportunities to apply academic knowledge and gradually become familiar with application design on cloud computing platforms.

However, I also recognize that I need to continue developing my technical competence, communication skills, teamwork abilities, and problem-solving skills to better meet the requirements of a professional working environment.

During the remaining period of the internship, I will continue building on my strengths while addressing areas that need improvement to enhance my work quality and achieve my learning objectives.

## 6. Conclusion

Conducting this self-assessment has helped me better understand the knowledge and skills I have developed, as well as the areas that require further improvement.

For me, the First Cloud AI Journey program is not only an opportunity to study AWS services but also an environment for improving independent learning, problem-solving, and professional responsibility.

I will continue maintaining a growth-oriented mindset, practicing proactively, and learning from new experiences to strengthen my technical abilities and prepare for future opportunities in Information Technology.
