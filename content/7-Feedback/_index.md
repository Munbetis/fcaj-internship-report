# SHARING AND FEEDBACK

## Introduction

During my internship in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I have had opportunities to explore new concepts in cloud computing, learn about Amazon Web Services (AWS), and apply my knowledge to hands-on exercises and research projects.

In addition to developing technical knowledge, the program has helped me improve my ability to conduct independent research, solve problems, manage my work, and present what I have learned.

Through this feedback, I would like to reflect on my experiences, evaluate different aspects of the program, and provide suggestions that may help improve the internship experience for future participants.

---

## 1. Overall Evaluation

### 1.1. Working Environment

During my participation in FCAJ, I have had the opportunity to become familiar with a learning and working environment focused on practical technologies, particularly Cloud Computing.

One aspect I appreciate is that the program encourages interns to actively explore new concepts, practice independently, and solve problems they encounter. This approach has helped me gradually develop the habit of reading technical documentation and searching for solutions rather than relying only on passive learning.

In addition to in-person activities, combining self-study with assigned tasks has helped me improve my self-discipline and time management skills.

However, for students who are new to AWS, the amount of technical knowledge and terminology can be challenging. Therefore, I believe that additional introductory sessions or experience-sharing activities among interns could make the learning process more approachable.

### 1.2. Support from Mentors and Team Administrators

Guidance from mentors and support from team administrators are important factors throughout the internship program.

For technical topics, having learning directions, reference materials, and opportunities to ask questions helps me identify the areas that require further study.

Additionally, information about internship procedures, report requirements, and expected progress helps me organize my tasks more effectively.

I believe that, beyond explaining technical concepts, encouraging interns to explore and test solutions independently is especially valuable. This process allows students to develop independent thinking and technical problem-solving skills.

I hope the program will continue maintaining communication channels and consider organizing additional topic-based question-and-answer sessions to help students overcome common challenges.

### 1.3. Relevance of the Internship to My Academic Major

In my opinion, the FCAJ program is closely related to Information Technology while also introducing practical knowledge that students may not have explored in depth during university courses.

During the internship, I have studied AWS services such as Amazon DynamoDB, AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, and Amazon S3. These technologies are connected to academic subjects including databases, programming, system design, and application development.

In particular, while researching the **Cloud Library Management System** project, I have had the opportunity to explore how multiple AWS services can be combined to design the architecture of a library management application.

Through this process, I have realized that application development involves more than writing source code. It also requires making decisions about service selection, data organization, security, scalability, and operating costs.

These experiences have helped me better understand how academic knowledge can be applied to practical problems.

### 1.4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

This is one of the aspects I value most about participating in FCAJ.

In terms of technical knowledge, I have had opportunities to learn more about cloud computing and Serverless architecture on AWS.

While studying **Amazon DynamoDB**, I explored NoSQL data organization, key design, and database operations. Practicing with book-related data also helped me better understand how these concepts could be applied to the Cloud Library project.

In addition, while preparing technical blog posts, I researched **AWS Cost Optimization**, cost monitoring tools, and **Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**.

Beyond technical knowledge, the program has helped me develop skills such as:

- Searching for, reading, and summarizing technical documentation.
- Analyzing requirements and identifying possible solutions.
- Performing hands-on exercises and verifying results step by step.
- Using Git and GitHub to manage project content.
- Presenting technical knowledge through reports and blog posts.
- Managing time and tracking work progress.
- Communicating and collaborating during project research.

I believe these skills will remain valuable not only during the internship but also throughout my academic and professional development.

### 1.5. Team Culture and Collaboration

During the program, I have recognized the importance of knowledge sharing and collaboration with other learners.

Especially when studying unfamiliar technologies, discussing different approaches and sharing practical difficulties can help participants gain a clearer understanding of technical problems.

While researching the Cloud Library Management System as a group project, I have also recognized the importance of establishing shared goals, dividing responsibilities, and maintaining communication so that different system components can work together.

Through this experience, I have learned that teamwork involves more than completing individual assignments. It also requires coordination, progress updates, and supporting one another when necessary.

I hope the program will continue creating opportunities for networking, experience sharing, and discussions among interns to strengthen collaboration and collective learning.

### 1.6. Internship Policies and Support Conditions

From my perspective, one of the most important aspects of an internship program is having a learning roadmap, reference materials, and clear evaluation requirements that allow students to prepare proactively.

Components such as Worklog, Proposal, Blogs, and Workshop provide directions for recording progress, researching technical topics, and developing practical projects.

However, because several requirements must be completed during the internship period, students need to establish appropriate learning and time management plans.

I believe the program could provide additional support through a checklist of tasks organized by stage, along with clear deadlines and assessment criteria.

For hands-on exercises involving AWS resources, I would also appreciate more guidance on budget monitoring, appropriate resource usage, and environment cleanup to reduce the risk of unexpected charges.

---

## 2. Additional Questions

### 2.1. What Have I Enjoyed Most During My Internship?

What I have enjoyed most is the opportunity to explore AWS technologies through practical exercises and application-oriented research.

Previously, when studying databases or software development, I mainly approached technical knowledge through individual university subjects. During FCAJ, I began to understand how cloud services can be combined to form a complete system.

For example, while practicing with Amazon DynamoDB and researching the Cloud Library architecture, I gained a better understanding of the relationships among databases, backend services, and other application components.

I also feel motivated by being able to use the knowledge I have gained to prepare technical articles, develop documentation, and gradually improve the project materials.

For me, the greatest value has been the improvement in my independent learning mindset and my ability to approach unfamiliar technologies.

### 2.2. What Should the Program Improve for Future Interns?

I believe the program could improve the experience for new interns by providing a more detailed hands-on learning roadmap during the initial stages.

For students with limited AWS experience, learning new services, completing practical exercises, and preparing internship reports at the same time can make it difficult to determine priorities.

Therefore, I would suggest:

- Providing a comprehensive checklist of required tasks.
- Offering illustrative examples for components such as Proposal and Workshop.
- Organizing additional sessions to address common technical issues.
- Providing guidance on monitoring and controlling AWS costs.
- Establishing progress checkpoints so students can identify areas that require improvement.

I believe these improvements would help interns become more proactive and spend more time focusing on learning and hands-on practice.

### 2.3. Would I Recommend This Internship Program to My Friends? Why?

I would consider recommending the FCAJ program to friends who are interested in Cloud Computing, AWS, and improving their independent learning skills.

One valuable aspect of the program is that participants are encouraged not only to study theoretical concepts but also to practice, research documentation, and prepare concrete technical deliverables.

Through these activities, students can gradually become familiar with approaches to problem-solving in cloud computing.

However, I would also explain that the program requires initiative, independent research, and effective time management because there are several topics and assignments to complete.

I believe it is a suitable experience for students who are willing to dedicate time to hands-on learning and seriously develop their AWS knowledge.

---

## 3. Suggestions and Expectations

### 3.1. What Suggestions Do I Have for Improving the Internship Experience?

Based on my experience participating in the program, I would like to provide several suggestions.

**First, provide a stage-based hands-on learning roadmap.**

A roadmap with smaller objectives and clearly defined expected outcomes would make it easier for new interns to track their progress.

**Second, increase practical guidance and technical Q&A sessions.**

In particular, discussions about common AWS configuration errors, IAM permissions, and service integrations would help students save time during the learning process.

**Third, provide additional guidance on AWS cost management.**

This is important so that interns can practice more confidently, understand how to configure budget alerts, and review resources after completing exercises.

**Fourth, create more opportunities for interns to exchange knowledge and experiences.**

Knowledge-sharing sessions, progress presentations, and technical discussions would help participants learn from different approaches to problem-solving.

### 3.2. Would I Like to Continue Participating in This Program in the Future?

If opportunities and circumstances allow, I would like to continue participating in AWS-related learning activities or advanced programs in the future.

I am particularly interested in Serverless architecture, database design on AWS, cost optimization, and applying Generative AI to practical software systems.

The knowledge I have gained during the internship provides a foundation for continued research and more advanced hands-on practice.

I also hope to have more opportunities to exchange knowledge with experienced professionals and participate in AWS community activities.

### 3.3. Additional Comments and Reflections

For me, participating in FCAJ has provided not only knowledge of AWS services but also a better understanding of the importance of proactive learning and persistence in problem-solving.

When approaching unfamiliar technologies, I have realized that encountering errors or struggling to understand a concept is a natural part of the learning process. What matters most is knowing how to search for documentation, verify each step, and learn from previous challenges.

Researching and developing the Cloud Library Management System project has also helped me recognize that a software system must be considered from multiple perspectives, including functionality, data, security, scalability, and cost.

I appreciate the opportunity to participate in the program and hope FCAJ will continue developing opportunities for more students to gain practical knowledge and skills in cloud computing.

---

## 4. Conclusion

Through my participation in the First Cloud AI Journey program, I have had opportunities to expand my knowledge of AWS and improve skills that are valuable for academic and professional development.

Research activities, hands-on practice, project planning, and technical blog preparation have helped me better understand how theoretical knowledge can be connected to practical applications.

Alongside these positive experiences, I believe that clearer learning roadmaps, appropriate technical support, and more opportunities for communication among interns could further improve the program.

I hope my reflections and suggestions can make a small contribution toward improving the learning and internship experiences of future FCAJ participants.
